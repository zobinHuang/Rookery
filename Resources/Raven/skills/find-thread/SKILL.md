# Find a thread

1. Search the likely ways: `search_mail` with `from` and then `to` for a person (their name or address), with `query` for a subject's words; widen `after` if nothing turns up, and look in `folder: sent` for what the user wrote. Search every account unless the user named one.
2. `read_thread` on the best match to see the whole exchange, and `read_mail` on the messages that carry its substance.
3. Answer with where it stands: who said what, in order, briefly; what is open and who it waits on; dates; and the account. Quote a line only where the wording matters.
